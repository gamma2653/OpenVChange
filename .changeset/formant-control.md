Add formant control. Shifting the pitch used to shift the resonances of the voice along with it, which is what makes a shifted voice sound like a tape played at the wrong speed. The new "Keep formants when shifting pitch" option moves them back, and the new Formant slider moves them on their own: down sounds like a larger person, up like a smaller one. The Deep voice, Giant, and Higher voice presets now use both. While formant control is in use it adds about 20 ms of delay. Both settings are off by default, so existing presets sound as before.
