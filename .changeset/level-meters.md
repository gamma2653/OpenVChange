Replace the level display with proper meters. There is now one for the input and one for the output, both on a decibel scale from -60 dB to full scale, with a marker that holds the highest recent peak and a readout in dB. A red light on the input meter shows that the microphone signal is clipping. A red light on the output meter shows that the effects are pushing the signal past full scale, which is when it starts to distort.
